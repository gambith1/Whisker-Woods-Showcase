# 🐾 Whisker Woods

**Build Your Farm. Explore New Worlds. Create Your Own Story.**

Welcome to **Whisker Woods**, a colorful pixel-art farming, life simulation and exploration RPG currently in active development using Godot 4.

Create your dream farm, restore abandoned facilities, raise animals, build friendships and discover a vast interconnected world filled with mysteries, adventures and unforgettable experiences.

## 🌿 Key Features

- **Farming & Greenhouse:** Cultivate crops, develop your farmland and restore an abandoned greenhouse.
- **Animal Care & Genetics:** Raise animals with individual personalities, needs and genetic traits.
- **Exploration:** Discover forests, deserts, snowy lands, volcanic regions, tropical islands and prehistoric wilderness.
- **Crafting & Production:** Gather resources, restore workshops and build production facilities.
- **NPC Relationships:** Meet unique characters, develop friendships and uncover their stories.
- **Quests & Progression:** Experience an extensive quest system spanning 100 character levels.
- **Multiplayer:** Planned cooperative gameplay to share your adventures with friends.
